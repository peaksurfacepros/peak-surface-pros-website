Not sure which service you need?

Detailing, paint correction, or ceramic coating?

Most people ask for "a detail," but what they need can be different. Here's the short version.

Ask us which one fits
Detailing
The standard deep clean: vacuum, shampoo, interior surfaces, wheels, a hand wash, and a protective sealant. Choose this if your car is dirty and you want it clean inside and out.
Paint correction
Detailing cleans the paint, and paint correction repairs it. If you have swirl marks, light scratches, or oxidation that won't wash off, it needs machine polishing.
Ceramic coating
A long-lasting protective layer applied after the paint is corrected. It makes the car easier to wash, resists water spots, and keeps its gloss far longer than wax.

Common questions about detailing and exterior cleaning in Yuba-Sutter

How much does mobile detailing cost in Yuba City?

Detailing starts at $65 for an Exterior Refresh, $129 for a Mini Detail, and $279 for a Full Detail. Ceramic coating starts at $795. Midsize SUVs and trucks add $20, and large SUVs, vans, and 3-row vehicles add $40. Heavily soiled vehicles may cost more. Text a photo or two of your vehicle to (530) 237-2741, or send them through the form, and we'll confirm your final price before we start.

Do I need to provide water or power for mobile detailing?

No. We bring our own water and power, so we can work in a driveway, an apartment lot, or an office parking lot.

Do I need to be home during my detail?

For an exterior detail, no. Just let us know where the car is parked. For interior work, we need the car unlocked or the keys, and we'll confirm an arrival window with you ahead of time.

What's the difference between a car wash event and mobile detailing?

Car wash events are quick exterior hand washes at set prices, done in a parking lot while you shop or work. Mobile detailing is a deeper clean, inside and out, done at your home or office by appointment.

How long does a wash take at an event?

Most washes are done in about the time it takes you to shop or finish a workout. Deluxe washes and interior add-ons take a little longer.

How much do pressure washing, gutter cleaning, solar panel cleaning, and holiday lights cost?

Every home is different, so we price these after learning about your property. Send the form with a short description or a few photos, and we'll get back to you with a price.

How often should solar panels be cleaned here?

For most Yuba-Sutter homes, twice a year: once in spring after pollen season, and once in late summer after harvest dust settles.

Can my business host a car wash event?

Yes. Choose "Host a wash event" in the form, tell us about your location, and we'll work out a date with you.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Yuba City, Marysville, Live Oak, Sutter, Tierra Buena, Linda, Olivehurst, Plumas Lake, Wheatland, and Browns Valley, plus Gridley, Biggs, Durham, and Chico to the north. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
